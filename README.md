# Claude from Zero to Hero: exercises

Exercises for the YouTube series **Claude from Zero to Hero** by Matthias Falland: Claude from the first chat to the step into Claude Code, in 9 parts (0 to 8).

Playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDeI72yRx3dk

## How to use

Each part has one folder. Open its `README.md`, do the task in your own Claude account, then compare your result with `solution/SOLUTION.md`. No Git needed: every folder is also available as a ZIP with a PDF solution sheet under [Releases](https://github.com/TheTrustedAdvisor/claude-from-zero-to-hero/releases).

| Part | Folder | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Claude for beginners: what it is and your first useful chat | [00-first-chat](00-first-chat) | Free |
| 1 How to prompt Claude: give it the job, not just the question | [01-prompt](01-prompt) | Free |
| 2 Claude with your files: PDFs, spreadsheets and checking the numbers | [02-files](02-files) | Free (step 4: Pro) |

More parts follow as the videos come out.
